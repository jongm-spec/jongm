Featured project

Phishing Awareness Training Manual

2026 · 42 pages

Phishing works because it fools people, not computers. So I built a training program to help BJU students spot it. I interviewed the university's Chief Operations & Technology Officer and one of our IT client services specialists, surveyed students, and looked at a real phishing attack that had hit our campus the year before. The result is a 42-page manual with learning objectives, a 40-minute training session, a "Phishing or Legit?" exercise, handouts, slides, and clear steps for what to do if you've already clicked.

Featured Work

Identifying and Responding to Phishing Attacks

Training manual (42 pages) · Security awareness · Needs assessment · Training design

Phishing goes after people rather than systems, and universities are frequent targets. I designed a full security awareness training program to teach college students how to spot phishing attempts and how to respond to them. I built it from real data: what I saw working in IT support, plus research I did with university IT leadership.

  • Needs assessment: interviewed the university's Chief Operations & Technology Officer and an IT Client Services Specialist, and surveyed students about how aware and confident they were
  • Real-incident grounding: analyzed a phishing campaign that impersonated the university and collected students' personal information
  • Training design: a 40-minute session on the ROPES model with measurable goals and objectives, a "Phishing or Legit?" exercise, and a transfer-of-training strategy
  • Core content: five warning signs of phishing, how to report a suspicious message, and what to do if you've already clicked
  • Deliverables: full trainer outline, handout materials, slide deck and promotional video
